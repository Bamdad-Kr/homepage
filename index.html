About

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate with a strong passion for computational approaches to the life sciences and a deep curiosity about how complex biological systems work. I'm interested in problems at the intersection of computer science, biology, and systems-level omics.

My contributions span noncoding RNA–cancer interaction prediction, interpretable neural-symbolic peptide modeling, and hypergraph learning for antimicrobial combination effects. I pair my CS background with self-directed study in biology, and I'm now looking for hands-on experimental experience alongside computational design.
